Your referral link

Other Magic Manager users can use this link to send you leads. Give it to your referral partners — they paste it onto your partner record in their app. When they refer someone to you, the lead lands in your Referral inbox (top of Leads) and you tap Add lead.

This is not your app address — it can only drop leads into your inbox. It can’t open your app or see anything in it.

Resetting makes every link you’ve already given out stop working — use it only if the link was shared by mistake.

Import clients from a spreadsheet

Have an existing client list? Export it as a CSV (in Excel or Google Sheets: File → Download / Save As → CSV), then upload it here. You'll match up the columns and review everything — nothing is added until you tap Import.

Any column layout works — you'll map them on the next screen. Starting from scratch? Handy columns: Customer name (the only required one), Phone, Email, Company, Service, Event type, Event date, Event location, Quoted price, Lead source, Status, Notes.

OR PASTE FROM A SPREADSHEET

No file needed — in Excel or Google Sheets, select your rows including the header row, copy, and paste below.

Match your columns

We guessed these from your headers — fix any that are wrong, and choose — Ignore — for columns you don't want.

Status maps to a fixed set. Magic Manager uses seven statuses — New, Pending, Booked, Completed, Lost, Referral, Referred. A mapped Status column is matched to these (case-insensitive), so “booked” becomes Booked; anything that doesn't match — or a blank, or no Status column — comes in as New. Only Booked and Completed count toward revenue in Insights.

Past events come in as Completed. Any booking whose event date is already in the past is brought in as Completed (a gig that's already happened) — except ones marked Lost, Referral, or Referred, which are kept as-is.
