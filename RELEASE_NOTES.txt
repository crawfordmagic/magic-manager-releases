What's new in this update:

• Calendar reminders now land on the right day and time. Follow-up and task reminders that sync to your Google Calendar could show up on the day before (around 8:00 PM) instead of the day they were due. That's fixed — a reminder with no specific time now appears at 9:00 AM on the correct day, and one with a time set shows at that exact time.
• December Rebooking — snooze a client. On the December Rebooking list, each client now has a "Snooze" button that hides them for this year's outreach without you having to contact them. They come back on their own in next year's rebooking cycle — handy for anyone you've decided not to reach out to this season.
