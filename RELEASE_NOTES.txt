What's new in this update:

• Saving a "Branded Gmail draft" now logs it the same way sending an email does — it shows up in that lead's contact history (with the usual "what happened?" prompt), a partner referral email gets logged and the lead marked as referred, and rebooking emails are logged too.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. (Your data is never affected.)

• OPTIONAL, only for Branded Gmail drafts: if you haven't already, also replace appsscript.json (in Apps Script, click the gear "Project Settings" and turn on "Show appsscript.json manifest file in editor"), save, then — BEFORE you deploy — pick the function named showGrantedScopes at the top and click Run, and approve what Google shows. Magic Manager only ever saves drafts to your Gmail — it never reads your mail and never sends anything to your clients on its own. Skip this and everything else works exactly as before.
