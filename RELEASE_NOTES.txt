Expenses, reimagined: the page now opens on a period selector (This Year by default, just like Insights) with a live six-badge summary — total spent, vs. last year, average per month, number of expenses, missing receipts, and top category — that recomputes as you switch periods. The Category and Receipt filters sit open right beneath it, and the Missing-receipts and Top-category badges are tappable to filter the list.

Also in this release: the Recycle Bin and Sync to Google Contacts are grouped into one tidy "Manage" section on the Leads screen, with deleted leads sorted by month and a one-tap "Empty" for each month; new leads start with Service, Event type, and Lead source blank so you choose them yourself; and a few layout refinements across the Leads screen (the year dropdown is now full-width, and the Manage section loads in cleanly after the page).
