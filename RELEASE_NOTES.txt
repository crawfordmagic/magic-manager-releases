What's new in this update:

• Deposits, your way — set your deposit as a percentage in Settings, or set it to 0 to skip deposits entirely so clients just pay the full balance.
• Make the contract yours — add your own cancellation policy and extra terms in Settings, and they appear on the agreement your clients sign.
• Share your W-9 and proof of insurance — upload them in Settings, and clients can view them right from their booking page.
• New: your own store — sell merch, gift cards, or downloads from a simple checkout page. Turn it on in Settings → Store (optional) and add your products; it stays off until you enable it.
• Send us feedback — a new "Send feedback" screen in Settings lets you report bugs and request features without leaving the app.
• Receipts now show your own business contact details.
• Cleaner imports — importing a client list no longer fills your Follow-Ups with old dates.
