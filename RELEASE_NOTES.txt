What's new in this update:

• NEW — Month filter in Insights. Next to "Period" there is now a "Month" menu. Pick a year, then a month, and everything in Insights follows it: your booked value, leads, sources, income, expenses, profit, the tax estimate, advertising and store sales. The monthly chart always shows the whole year, and the "New this week / New this month" boxes become "New in March (inquired)" and "Booked from them" when you pick a month. Months with nothing in them aren't listed.

• CHANGED — Tax prep totals. The "net" line is now "Total received minus expenses": everything clients paid you (including any card fee they added) minus your expenses, including the Stripe fees you entered. Before, it left the card fee out but still subtracted Stripe's cost, so it came out too low. If you haven't entered your Stripe fees yet, the net runs slightly high, and the report tells you so.

• CHANGED — Insights lists are now in a sensible order. Booked, In play, services and event types show the soonest show first; New, Lost and Referred show the newest inquiry first; a lead source shows the biggest quoted jobs first. Tapping a source under Best Performing Sources now lists only the leads for the period you picked, instead of every year.

• Cleaner behind the scenes: a few unused pieces of the app were removed. Nothing you use changes.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve.
