What's new in this update:

• Leads has a redesigned “Today” list: every follow-up, task, and payment waiting on you now shows as its own row — client name, what it is, and how overdue it is — sorted so the most urgent things are on top. Tap a row to act on it.

• Each lead now shows a “Next step” right under their name: generate the contract, get it signed, mark a deposit or balance received, or ask for a review — whichever applies. No more scrolling to the bottom to find the contract button.

• The client booking page now combines signing and paying the deposit into one step, and shows a clear “Signed / Deposit / Balance” status every time a client returns to it.

• A lead is now marked Booked automatically the moment the agreement is signed and the deposit is received (or no deposit is required) — you don’t have to set the status by hand anymore.

• Recycle Bin, Google Contacts sync, and “Export my clients” moved from the bottom of the Leads screen into Settings → Tools.

• Client portal polish: booking details (date, location, deposit/balance, total) stay visible after signing instead of disappearing, dates are spelled out in full, and “ACH” now reads “Bank transfer.”

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace ALL FIVE files together — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html, Store.html and appsscript.json — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. (Your data is never affected.)
