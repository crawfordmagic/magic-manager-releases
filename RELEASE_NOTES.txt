What's new in this update:

• CHANGED — A shorter "Getting paid" screen in Settings. Up front you now see just your deposit, the card fee, the two notification emails, and the simple payment handles (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Zelle). ACH bank transfer, Wire transfer, Mailed check and Cash are small rows that stay closed until you tap them, and each shows a check mark once it's set up. Nothing you've saved moves or changes.

• Also in the last two updates: an optional "30 days out" check-in (Settings → More options → Reminders & taxes, off until you switch it on); a shorter Settings screen with three sections up front and everything optional behind "More options"; follow-up reminders that show where the event stands; "Add extra to balance" on a booked lead; Compare with last year or any other year in Insights and Expenses; and a cleaner black-and-gold look for dropdowns, dates and times.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve.
