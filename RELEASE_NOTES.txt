What's new in this update:

• NEW — A gentle check-in for partners who are not on Magic Manager. A week after you refer a lead to a partner who has no Magic Manager link saved, you get one reminder (to you, never to them) with Call and Message buttons, and the choices They booked it / Didn't book / Ask again in 2 weeks. Partners who use Magic Manager are never asked about: their app tells yours by itself.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve.
