What's new in this update:

• Cash is now a payment option. Record a cash payment on any lead, and — if you want — offer Cash on your client page (Settings → Getting paid → Cash instructions; leave it blank to keep it off).

• Payments flow in, with you in control. When a client taps "I've sent my payment" on their portal, you get a heads-up email and a "Payments to confirm" card in the app — nothing is marked received until you confirm it actually arrived. Card payments are the exception: they auto-confirm the moment Stripe verifies them, so those need no tap at all. (You can turn the emails off under Settings → Getting paid.)

• Set the balance payment method yourself. Your lead now has both a deposit and a balance payment-method field, so you can record each one separately when a client pays them different ways.

• A tidier client page. Your event portal now shows only the payment methods you've actually set up — and Credit Card appears only once your Stripe is connected — so clients never see an option you can't accept.
