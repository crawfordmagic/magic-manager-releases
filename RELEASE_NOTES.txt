What's new in this update:

• NEW — Event profit. Open a booked lead and tap Event profit to see what the show brought in, what it cost, and what you kept. On Expenses, each expense can be linked to an event, or you can add or link one right from the card. Linking is only a tag: it never changes your expense totals.

• NEW — Trips and leave times. Each show now has “Leaving from” and “Traveling” fields. Leaving from sets where your drive starts, so the leave time and the “Leave in…” countdown on Today are right when you are coming from somewhere else, and they say where from. Set Traveling to Yes for a flight and the leave time is skipped. On a long-drive show, if you have a hotel on your Google Calendar within an hour of the venue, the lead offers it as your starting point with one tap.

• IMPROVED — Add Lead now has every question the website inquiry form can ask. Audience age range and Start time are added under Event details, and the website form can ask for both too.

• IMPROVED — Pages refresh when you come back. The event portal rechecks payment and signature when a client returns to the tab, and the app reloads your data quietly after you have been away a few minutes.

• IMPROVED — Money lines keep their cents ($42.50, not $43) in Insights and the expense lists. Totals stay in whole dollars. On Referred, each partner shows the fees received and the fees expected.

• IMPROVED — The contract fee table says “Balance” instead of “Balance (Remaining)”, so a paid contract no longer looks unpaid. Contracts already signed are not changed.

• IMPROVED — The Expense event picker lists the event closest to today first, “Duplicate as new booking” takes two taps like Delete, and the event portal’s per-client note is labelled “Booking details”. This “What’s new” box also now starts folded.

• Still new if you are updating from before: the website inquiry form and TRMNL display (both optional and off until you switch them on), a client can have their own contract, booked value includes travel fees, and you can turn on a nightly Google Contacts sync.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve.
