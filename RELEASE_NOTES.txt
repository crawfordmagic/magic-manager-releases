What's new in this update:

• Insights now filter by year everywhere. Choosing a year at the top of Insights updates every section — Best Performing Sources, Advertising ROI, Store Sales, referrals, and the rest — not just the summary tiles at the top. Advertising ROI now shows the year you picked (no more confusing year labels), and a "Showing: <year>" line under the picker makes the active year clear. Choose "All Time" to compare every year side by side.
• Your clients' event portal: the "View the full agreement (PDF)" link is now a clear button with an icon, matching the other document buttons on the page.
