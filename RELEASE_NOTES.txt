What's new in this update:

• Snappier reminders. Tapping "Mark done" on a follow-up — and Confirm/Dismiss on a reported payment — now responds instantly instead of pausing while it saves.

• More accurate receipts. Receipts now reflect the real date a payment was made (not just when you confirmed it), and updating a payment method on an already-paid lead refreshes its receipt automatically — without changing the payment date. Card payments also now double-check that the exact expected amount was collected before marking them received.
