What's new in this update:

• The lead page now follows the life of the booking: while someone is still a prospect, the Follow-up and Status tiles and the History bar sit right at the top; once they're a client, the Signed → Deposit → Balance tracker leads. The Follow-up and Status tiles are more compact, and "Mark as reference" waits until after the event.

• One small, quiet dropdown for periods: Leads (beside Archive), Insights and Expenses now share the same subtle pill picker, with a "Period" label on Insights. The redundant "Showing…" line on Insights is gone, and on Expenses the Period and Month pickers now sit side by side.

• Dropdown lists are now dark with light text everywhere (the Status list used to open white and was hard to read).

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace ALL FIVE files together — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html, Store.html and appsscript.json — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. (Your data is never affected.)
