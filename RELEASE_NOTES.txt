What's new in this update:

• Security: your bank account and routing numbers (if you use Bank Transfer or Wire) are no longer included in the public contract and store pages. They show up only after a client opens their own contract link, so your clients see the same payment instructions as before.

• Security: text typed by clients and store visitors (names, reviews, payment method) can no longer be used to slip a spreadsheet formula into your sheet.

• Client receipt links repair themselves. If a receipt file is ever moved to the Trash or deleted, the link is restored or rebuilt automatically, so a client never lands on a "file was deleted" page.

• Your scheduled background jobs can now only be started by Google's own timer or by you.

• Card payments are checked more strictly: a payment is only marked received automatically if Stripe confirms it was made for that exact contract and payment. Anything else is recorded as "reported" for you to confirm, as before.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace ALL FIVE files together — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html, Store.html and appsscript.json — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Missing Sign.html in particular would hide the Bank Transfer / Wire options from your clients. (Your data is never affected.)
