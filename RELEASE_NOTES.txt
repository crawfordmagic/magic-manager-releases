What's new in this update:

• Fixed a date bug. Event dates and follow-up dates no longer default to a strange old year (1902) — the date picker now opens on today, as it should.

• Faster client import. You can now paste your client list straight from a spreadsheet — select your rows in Excel or Google Sheets, copy, and paste, with no CSV export step. (The file upload is still there if you prefer it.)

• Small polish. Tidied the Leads → Manage section, and the "What's changed" history in Help stays neat as updates add up.
