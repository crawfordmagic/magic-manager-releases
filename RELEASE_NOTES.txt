What's new in this update:

• Expenses are easier to scan. In the year view, expenses are grouped by month — each month is a single row showing how many expenses it holds and the month's total. Tap a month to open it. (Pick a single month and you still get the plain list.)

• A more consistent look. The collapsible sections on Leads and Expenses now use the same rows as Insights, so everything looks and taps the same way.
