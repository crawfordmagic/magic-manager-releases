What's new in this update:

• Send a lead to another Magic Manager user in one tap. When you refer a lead to a partner who also uses Magic Manager, your referral email can now include a link that drops the lead into their app — no retyping. It lands in a new "Referral inbox" on their Leads screen, and they tap Add lead (nothing is added until they do). To set it up, each person copies their link from Settings → Referral link, and the other pastes it onto that partner's record. See Help → "Sending a lead to another Magic Manager user."

• Fixed a display bug. Some green "success" elements had lost their color — including the "Confirm received" button on payments waiting for your confirmation, which was hard to see. They're back to normal.
