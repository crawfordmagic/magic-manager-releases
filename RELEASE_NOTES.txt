What's new in this update:

• NEW — Referral fees. When a partner pays you a fee for a lead you referred, record it right on that lead (the Referral fees box under "Referred to"): the amount, the date, and whether it's received or still expected. Each partner's record lists their fees with totals. Fees you mark received count as income in Insights and appear in your tax prep package.

• NEW — A note for one contract. The new "Contract note" field on a lead prints a line in that contract's terms and shows it on the event portal right beside the amounts (for example, "Travel to be confirmed and added to the final balance"). Your client sees this text, and if you add or change a note after a contract was made, the app reminds you to regenerate it.

• NEW — The Oracle can answer "Where is my money going?" (your biggest expense categories for the period you're viewing), and it will mention a referral fee that has been waiting more than two weeks.

• CHANGED — Settings opens instantly instead of loading every time.

• CHANGED — Emailing or texting a partner: new referrals come first, then your latest referred leads, with "Show older" for the rest. The Oracle's question buttons also line up in even rows now.

• CHANGED — Bottom bar: the icon of the tab you tap pops slightly, and tapping Leads while you're already on it takes you home. It closes anything open, clears the search, returns to the Follow up list and scrolls to the top.

• FIXED — The Send Feedback form showed a white background instead of dark on some phones. It now matches the rest of the app.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve. A "Referral Fees" sheet and a "Contract Note" column are added to your Google Sheet automatically the first time you use them.
