What's new in this update:

• Make the client portal yours. New "Client portal look" in Settings lets you set an accent color, a light or custom-color background, and a font pairing for the page your clients see — with a live preview. Leave it alone and you keep the classic dark-and-gold look.

• Sources and event types, in sync. A lead's Source is now a dropdown of your Lead Sources, and both Source and Event Type have "+ Add new…" — type a new one on a lead and it's added to your Settings lists automatically (no duplicates).

• Know the moment a client signs. You now get an email as soon as a client signs their contract (on by default; turn it off under Getting paid).

• Review reminder, spelled out. The day after an event, your follow-up now reads "Reach out to the client for a review through their event portal," with a one-tap Email button.

• Cleaner Today card and Insights. Longer names no longer spill outside the box, and the Insights rows have breathing room.

• Heads-up if file sharing is blocked. If your Google account blocks public sharing, the app now warns you instead of sending clients a link they can't open.

• Client portal tweak: after the event date, the pre-event note is replaced by the review request.
