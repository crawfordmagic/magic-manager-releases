What's new in this update:

• Help is reorganized into two simple, alphabetized lists — “Using Magic Manager” and “Troubleshooting” — instead of six sections. Your version, update status, and “What's changed” history moved to their own screen: Settings → About Magic Manager.

• “Your settings” (business info, payment methods, contract terms, etc.) now collapses to a single row on the Settings screen, so it's much shorter at a glance. Tap it to expand — your choice is remembered on your device.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace ALL FIVE files together — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html, Store.html and appsscript.json — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. (Your data is never affected.)
