What's new in this update:

• Your signature on contracts and receipts: in Settings → Your business → Your signature, pick a signature style (Dancing Script, Mr De Haviland, Mr Dafoe or Qwigley) with a live preview, or upload a picture of your own signature. An uploaded signature is kept private in your Google Drive and is used instead of the style. New contracts and receipts use it; ones you've already sent don't change.

• Tip: if your logo is on a see-through background and you'd rather not have the black square behind it on contracts and receipts, turn off Settings → Advanced → Logo backing.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace ALL FIVE files together — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html, Store.html and appsscript.json — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. (Your data is never affected.)
