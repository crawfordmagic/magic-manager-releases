What's new in this update:

• Transparent logos look right on your paperwork. If your logo has a see-through background, Magic Manager now puts a solid black square behind it on your contracts and payment receipts, so it doesn't look odd on white paper. Logos that aren't transparent are left exactly as they were. Prefer your logo untouched? Turn it off under Settings → Advanced → Logo backing. (Contracts you've already generated aren't changed.)

• A new font for your client portal. "Clean — Open Sans" is now an option under Settings → Client portal look, alongside the other font choices.
