What's new in this update:

• Your Event Portal — the page your clients use to review, sign, and pay is now "Your Event Portal," a cleaner home for everything tied to their booking. (Existing links still work.)
• Documents at their fingertips — the Event Portal now has a "Your Documents" section with one-tap buttons to view the signed agreement, deposit and balance receipts, your W-9, and proof of insurance. Each button appears only when that document actually exists.
• New lead field: Entertainment Budget — track what a client has budgeted for entertainment, shown right next to your quote. Reorder or hide it like any field.
• New payment options — accept PayPal and Zelle for deposits, balances, and store sales.
• Card fee, your call — the 3.25% credit-card processing fee is now optional. Switch it off in Settings → Payments to absorb it yourself.
• Track your store sales — record who bought from your store (including sales made outside the app) and see that money in your Insights income.
• Snooze update reminders — snooze this "update available" notice for a week or a month, or skip a version; your choice is remembered across your devices.
• Plus a collapsible recurring-expenses list and a mobile store-checkout fix.
