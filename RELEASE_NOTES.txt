What's new in this update:

• Insights drill-downs stay in Insights. Tapping a lead source, service, event type, referral, or lost reason now opens the details right there in the Insights tab — with a back button — instead of jumping over to your Leads list. The monthly income/expense trend and the expense breakdown are now tappable too: tap a month to see that month's income and expenses, or tap a category to see the expenses behind it.
• Settings opens tidy. The Settings screen now starts with every section collapsed, so you see the whole list at a glance and expand just the one you need.
