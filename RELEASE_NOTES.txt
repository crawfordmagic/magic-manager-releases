What's new in this update:

• Message templates are easier to find. Add a ready-made starter set with one tap, manage everything under Settings → Message templates, and get a short hint the first few times you open the email/text pickers. (Your existing templates are never changed.)

• Settings is reorganized by purpose — Tools, Your settings, Help — and "Getting paid" is now separate from "Payment methods".

• Adding a lead is instant. It saves in the background, and if something goes wrong your details come back in the form.

• Add form: "Private Event" is now a default event type, and Event type and Lead source have "+ Add new…".

• Follow-ups are clearer: a "Due today" section, an "Old follow-ups" group you can clear in one tap, and a History pill on Follow-Ups Due that shows your recent contacts right on the card. Cards in the Today summary open directly under their row.

• Fixes: the date field no longer jumps away while you type, the follow-up time popup no longer moves the date to today, and Oracle no longer says nothing is overdue when booked clients have follow-ups due.
