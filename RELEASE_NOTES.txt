What's new in this update:

• New payment options — accept PayPal and Zelle for deposits, balances, and store sales. Turn them on in Settings by adding your PayPal.Me handle or Zelle email/phone.
• Card fee, your call — the 3.25% credit-card processing fee is now optional. Leave it on (the default) or switch it off in Settings → Payments to absorb it yourself, so card clients pay the plain amount.
• Track your store sales — record who bought from your store, including sales you made outside the app, and see that money in your Insights income. Pick the product from a dropdown, mark it received, and edit the amount if you gave a discount. (Shows up only when your store is on.)
• Snooze update reminders — snooze this "update available" notice for a week or a month, or skip a version entirely. Your choice is remembered across your devices, so no more nagging every time you open the app.
• Tidier expenses — your recurring expenses now tuck into a collapsible list.
• Fix — store checkout now works reliably on phones.
