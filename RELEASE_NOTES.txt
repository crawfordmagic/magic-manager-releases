What's new in this update:

• Security: your leads, contacts and settings are now protected on the server by your private app key, not just hidden on the page. Nothing changes in how you use the app, and the links you send clients (contracts, payments, store) work exactly as before.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace ALL FIVE files together — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html, Store.html and appsscript.json — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. If a file is missed, the app now shows an "Update not finished" message telling you what to replace (your data is never affected).
