What's new in this update:

• NEW (optional) — Branded Gmail draft. On a lead, open Email and tap "Branded Gmail draft" under a template: a polished email lands in your Gmail drafts, with your event portal link as a button and your signature (logo, name, business, phone, website, email) at the bottom. For a partner email with a referral lead attached, the one-tap link becomes an "Add to Magic Manager" button. This needs one extra update step — see the OPTIONAL note below. Until you do it, the button stays hidden and nothing else changes.

• Settings → "Event portal & email look": your accent color and background now style those emails too. Emails use the closest font every mail app has, because mail apps can't load web fonts.

• Settings → Tools → "Send me a test email" checks that your alerts (a client signs, a payment is reported) can reach you, and tells you plainly if something is blocking them.

• Leads: when some upcoming shows fall in another year, a quiet line under Upcoming Shows says so (for example "5 this year · 1 in 2027").

• Fixed: an unexpected font value in the event portal look could stop the portal from loading.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. (Your data is never affected.)

• OPTIONAL, only for Branded Gmail drafts: ALSO replace appsscript.json (in Apps Script, click the gear "Project Settings" and turn on "Show appsscript.json manifest file in editor"), save, then — BEFORE you deploy — pick the function named showGrantedScopes at the top and click Run, and approve what Google shows. The screen will be longer than before and includes Gmail ("Manage drafts and send emails"); Magic Manager only ever saves drafts to your Gmail — it never reads your mail and never sends anything to your clients on its own. Then deploy the new version. Skip all of this and everything else works exactly as before.
