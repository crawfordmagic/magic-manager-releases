What's new in this update:

• A built-in Help center. Tap Settings → Help & guides for step-by-step walkthroughs (updating the app, connecting your website forms), quick fixes for common questions, and your current version with a "What's changed" history — all in one place.

• A cleaner, more consistent look. Insights, Expenses, and the lead-management tools now share one tidy, tap-to-expand list style, so the whole app feels the same and is easier to scan. Insights opens each section — Lead Source, Best-Performing Sources, Money, and the rest — as a neat card that expands to its chart with a tap.

• Export your clients. A new "Export my clients (CSV)" option (Leads → Manage) gives you a one-tap backup of your whole client list — the companion to Import.

• Easier importing. "Bring in your existing clients" now shows up right where you'd look for it — including a friendly prompt when your account is new — and stays out of the way once you're rolling.

• Small polish and fixes throughout.
