What's new in this update:

• Faster everyday actions: logging a call, text or email note, and adding, editing or deleting an expense now show instantly and save in the background. If a save ever fails, it's undone with a message and nothing you typed is lost.

• Faster client portal: a client's link opens with their booking right away (no more "Loading your agreement…"), signing goes straight to the deposit step, and "I've sent my deposit" and the feedback form confirm instantly. The signed PDF is prepared in the background and appears under Your Documents a few seconds later.

• If a signed contract PDF ever can't be created, you now get an email about it (before, it failed silently).

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace ALL FIVE files together — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html, Store.html and appsscript.json — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. (Your data is never affected.)
