What's new in this update:

• NEW, optional — a "30 days out" check-in. In Settings → More options → Reminders & taxes, switch on "Remind me 30 days before each show". New bookings then get a "30 days out — Touch base with the client" reminder in your Today list, ahead of the 1-week check-in. A show booked less than 30 days ahead skips it, and shows you've already booked are left exactly as they are. It's off until you turn it on.

• CHANGED — Settings is shorter. Instead of eleven sections, you see three up front — Your business (with your contact details), Getting paid (with your payment methods) and Services & event portal — and everything optional is tucked behind one "More options" row: Contract terms, Store, Reminders & taxes, and Advanced. Nothing you've saved moves or changes.

• CHANGED — Follow-up reminders tell you more. A reminder for a booked client now shows where the event stands ("Event in 30 days · Nov 7, 2026 · Corporate"), and a follow-up date you set yourself reads "Follow-up — Touch base with the client" instead of just "Follow-up".

• Also in the last two updates: "Add extra to balance" on a booked lead (for an on-site extension or add-on), Compare with last year or any other year in Insights and Expenses, attaching an already-referred lead when you follow up with a partner, one-tap field buttons in template editors, and a cleaner black-and-gold look for dropdowns, dates and times.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve.
