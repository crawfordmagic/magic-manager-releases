What's new in this update:

• NEW — Event time zone. For a show in a different time zone, pick the zone on the lead (the new "Event time zone" field, right after End). Enter the start and end times as the clock at the venue, and the contract states them with the zone, for example "6:00 PM Pacific (PDT)", and your client emails say "6:00 PM Pacific time". Leave it blank and everything stays exactly as before. Regenerate a contract after setting a zone so it picks it up. For now your Google Calendar entry and "Leave for" alert still use your own time zone, so for a show in another zone, check the calendar time.

• NEW — Type a time instead of scrolling. The time picker now has a box where you can type "5:30 PM" (or 5:30pm, 530p, 17:30) and press Enter. On a computer the cursor goes straight into it; on a phone the wheels are still the quick way in.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve. An "Event Time Zone" column is added to your Google Sheet automatically the first time you set a zone.
