What's new in this update:

• NEW (OPTIONAL) — Website inquiry form. If you have a contact form on your own website, people who fill it out can now become new leads in Magic Manager automatically, and you get an email each time. It is off until you switch it on: Settings → More options → Website inquiry form. Tick the questions you want, tap Copy code, paste the code into an HTML or Embed block on your website and publish, then tap Send a test inquiry. Entries arrive marked New, from Website, with a follow-up due today. It has built-in spam protection, and “Make a new key” cancels an old code if you ever need to. A new Help topic walks through it. Watch the walkthrough video: https://youtu.be/gdQGBwn0Y8E

• NEW (OPTIONAL) — TRMNL e-ink display. If you own a TRMNL screen, Magic Manager can send it your next show, follow-ups, new leads, booked, owed, and income vs. expenses every 30 minutes. Settings → More options → TRMNL display.

• IMPROVED — Insights → Booked list now shows what is paid and what is still due for each show, with totals. Each show’s amount includes its travel fee.

• IMPROVED — Deposit and Balance now appear as small pills on one line under each show, in both Upcoming Shows and the Booked list. Tap one, then tap again to mark the payment received; the screen updates at once. A new Help topic explains it.

• IMPROVED — The travel fee is counted the same way everywhere: Insights drill-downs, the Oracle and in-play totals now match Booked value, and the lead screens say “total” where travel is included.

• IMPROVED — Upcoming follow-ups are grouped by day. The soonest day is open, the others are collapsed with a date badge, and the badges line up.

• IMPROVED — Settings is tidier. Settings → Tools is shorter: Import, Export and Google Contacts are now together in one row called “Your clients” (the nightly Contacts sync is there), and “Send me a test email” lives in Message templates. The sections inside More options are in alphabetical order.

• IMPROVED — Small touches: the Booked tiles in Insights match the other tiles, the Today card says when a client’s own contract is not yet signed, date badges line up in the to-do list, and the Postpone hint can be dismissed.

• Still new if you are updating from before: a client can have their own contract (Help explains it), booked value includes travel fees, and you can turn on a nightly Google Contacts sync.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve. The website inquiry form and the TRMNL display are both off until you switch them on.
