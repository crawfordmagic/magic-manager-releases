What's new in this update:

• NEW — Search your settings. At the top of Settings there's now a search box, the same one you know from Leads. Type a word like "deposit", "venmo" or "store" and only the matching settings show, with the sections that hold them opened for you. Tap the ✕ to clear it.

• CHANGED — A calmer, quicker Settings screen. Your settings now sit right on the screen instead of behind an extra tap, ahead of the Tools list. Each section shows a one-line summary under its name — for example "50% deposit · Card fee on · Venmo, Zelle" — so you can check a setting without opening it. A "Saved ✦" message pops up when a change saves, and fields open the right phone keyboard (email, phone number pad, web address, account numbers).

• CHANGED — Less small print. Help text that only repeated a setting's name is gone, and longer help shows its first sentence with a "More" link. Once your business is set up, the guided-setup button and the "Start with…" hint disappear from Settings; guided setup is always one tap away under Help → Guided setup.

• Also in the last few updates: a shorter "Getting paid" section (ACH, wire, check and cash collapse until you need them); an optional "30 days out" check-in (Settings → More options → Reminders & taxes); follow-up reminders that show where the event stands; "Add extra to balance" on a booked lead; Compare with last year or any other year in Insights and Expenses; and a cleaner black-and-gold look for dropdowns, dates and times.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve.
