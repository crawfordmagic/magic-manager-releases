What's new in this update:

• CHANGED — The contract section no longer has an "Edit in Google Docs" link. The contract is rebuilt from the lead's details every time you regenerate it (and when it is signed), so changes typed into the document were being lost. To change the date, place or price, edit the lead and tap Regenerate contract; extra wording goes in Contract note.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve.
