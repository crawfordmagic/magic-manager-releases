What's new in this update:

• Fixed: the event portal no longer shows the credit-card processing fee on its first screen when you don't offer card payments. Before, a client opening their event portal could see a deposit about 3% too high (for example $774.38 instead of $750.00) until they changed the payment menu. If you accept cards, nothing changes.

• A cleaner, more consistent look: the bottom tab that said "Add" now reads "New Lead", and the repeated page titles at the top are gone — the bottom tabs already show where you are, so every screen opens with your business name and gets straight to the content. The tab bar also fits small phones better.

• Branded email signature: your logo now opens your website when tapped (if you've entered a website in Settings), and Settings now says which details — logo, contact email, phone and website — appear in your branded email signature.

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. (Your data is never affected.)

• OPTIONAL, only for Branded Gmail drafts: if you haven't already, also replace appsscript.json (in Apps Script, click the gear "Project Settings" and turn on "Show appsscript.json manifest file in editor"), save, then — BEFORE you deploy — pick the function named showGrantedScopes at the top and click Run, and approve what Google shows. Magic Manager only ever saves drafts to your Gmail — it never reads your mail and never sends anything to your clients on its own. Skip this and everything else works exactly as before.
