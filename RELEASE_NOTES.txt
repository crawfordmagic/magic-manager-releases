What's new in this update:

• NEW — Arrive early, your way. Settings → Reminders & taxes now has “Arrive this many minutes early” (60 unless you change it) and “Arrive early by service”, a small box for each of your Services, for example 90 for a stage show and 30 for strolling. Your calendar reminder, the “Leave by” line and the show-day countdown all follow it. Leave everything blank and nothing changes.

• CHANGED — Expenses: “Export to a spreadsheet” now follows the Month you are viewing. Pick a year and a month and the file holds just that month (named like expenses-2026-10.csv). A year alone, or All time, works as before.

• CHANGED — Overdue follow-ups and search results now use the same compact one-line rows as the rest of the Leads page. Each row shows the name, price, status, event details and the follow-up date (red when overdue).

• IMPORTANT when updating: replace the four files as usual — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html and Store.html — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. Your data is never affected, and there are no new permissions to approve.
