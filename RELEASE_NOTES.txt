What's new in this update:

• One name everywhere: the page your clients use to sign, pay and see their booking is now called the "event portal" throughout the app, Settings and Help (it was sometimes the "client portal" or "booking page"). • IMPORTANT when updating: replace ALL FIVE files together — Code.gs, Index.html, Sign.html, Store.html and appsscript.json — then Deploy → Manage deployments → New version. (Your data is never affected.)
