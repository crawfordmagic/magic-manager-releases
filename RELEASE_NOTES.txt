What's new in this update:

• Guided setup — a friendly step-by-step guide walks you through the essentials: your business details and logo, how clients reach you, how you get paid, and your services — with a live preview of what clients will see. It opens automatically the first time you set up, and you can reopen it anytime from Settings → "Guided setup wizard." Everything stays optional, and the rest of your options are still right there in Settings.
• Your deposit percentage now applies everywhere — when you set a price on a booking, the deposit is calculated from the percent you choose in Settings → Getting paid (and now includes any travel fee), so what you see matches your contract, your client's payment page, and their card charge exactly. Deposits you've already saved on existing bookings are left untouched.
